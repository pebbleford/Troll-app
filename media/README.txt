Drop your own pictures, sounds and videos in this folder.
They show up automatically in the "Your own pictures, sounds & videos" section on the iPad.

Pictures: png, jpg, gif, webp, bmp   -> use as a jumpscare or just pop them up
Sounds:   mp3, wav, ogg, m4a, aac, flac
Videos:   mp4, webm, m4v (mov sometimes works too)

You can also upload straight from the iPad with the "Upload a file" button.
